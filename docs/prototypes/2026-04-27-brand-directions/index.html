What I pulled from your IG

three threads

Color DNA

Three palettes recur: deep navy → forest green diagonal gradients (Spotify, Giving Tuesday, Eid); warm peach/cream gradients (testimonial story); solid royal cobalt + gold + Islamic pattern (Ramadan, Eid). Cobalt blue is the through-line — already your app's brand color and on 4 of 6 images.

Type DNA

Two consistent moves: tall editorial serif in big caps for ceremonial moments ("YOUNG MUSLIMS", "EID MUBARAK") + Arabic calligraphy in gold for religious salutations. Body is always clean sans. The 2025 Coordinators graphic is the outlier — bold italic geometric sans, sports-team energy.

Decorative DNA

Islamic geometric patterns appear as corner frames (testimonial, Eid) and full backdrops (Ramadan), not edge-to-edge wallpaper. Mosque silhouettes are silhouettes-not-illustration. The YM logo M doubles as oversized watermark on stat-driven posts.
